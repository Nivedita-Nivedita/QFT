# Sources: gauge theory and four-manifolds reference library

A catalog of standard textbooks on the differential geometry underlying
gauge theory, and its applications to four-dimensional topology. None have
a legitimate copy in hand yet, and none has a chosen formalization target;
see [roadmap/gauge-theory-references/README.md](../../roadmap/gauge-theory-references/README.md).
Table-of-contents information below was gathered from each publisher's own
listing.

## Foundational texts

### Mathematical Gauge Theory: With Applications to the Standard Model of Particle Physics

Mark J.D. Hamilton. Universitext, Springer Cham, 2017. eISBN
978-3-319-68439-0. xviii+658pp.

11 chapters in three parts:

- **Mathematical Foundations**: 1. Lie Groups and Lie Algebras: Basic
  Concepts; 2. Lie Groups and Lie Algebras: Representations and Structure
  Theory; 3. Group Actions; 4. Fibre Bundles; 5. Connections and Curvature;
  6. Spinors.
- **The Standard Model of Elementary Particle Physics**: 7. The Classical
  Lagrangians of Gauge Theories; 8. The Higgs Mechanism and the Standard
  Model; 9. Modern Developments and Topics Beyond the Standard Model.
- **Appendices**: A. Background on Differentiable Manifolds; B. Background
  on Special Relativity and Quantum Field Theory.

### Principal Bundles (2 vols.)

Stephen Bruce Sontz. Universitext, Springer Cham.

- **Vol. 1, *The Classical Case*** (2015, eISBN 978-3-319-14765-9,
  xv+280pp). 16 chapters: Introduction; Basics of Manifolds; Vector
  Bundles; Vectors, Covectors, and All That; Exterior Algebra and
  Differential Forms; Lie Derivatives; Lie Groups; The Frobenius Theorem;
  Principal Bundles; Connections on Principal Bundles; Curvature of a
  Connection; Classical Electromagnetism; Yang-Mills Theory; Gauge Theory;
  The Dirac Monopole; Instantons; What Next?
- **Vol. 2, *The Quantum Case*** (2015, eISBN 978-3-319-15829-7, xv+350pp,
  non-commutative-geometry generalization): Introduction; First-Order
  Differential Calculus; First-Order Differential Calculus of a Hopf
  Algebra; Adjoint Co-action; Covariant Bimodules; Covariant First-Order
  Differential Calculi; The Braid Groups; An Interlude: Some Abstract
  Nonsense; The Braided Exterior Algebra; Higher-Order Differential Calculi;
  *-Structures; Quantum Principal Bundles; Finite Classical Groups; Dunkl
  Operators as Covariant Derivatives in a QPB; What Next?

### The Geometry of Physics: An Introduction

Theodore Frankel. Cambridge University Press, 3rd ed., 2011 (1st ed. 1997).
DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511817977.

Three parts (chapter-level detail pending a closer pass): **I. Manifolds,
Tensors, and Exterior Forms**; **II. Geometry and Topology**; **III. Lie
Groups, Bundles, and Chern Forms**; plus appendices on forms in continuum
mechanics, harmonic chains and Kirchhoff's circuit laws, and symmetries.

### Topology, Geometry and Gauge Fields (2 vols.)

Gregory L. Naber.

- **Vol. 1, *Foundations*** (Texts in Applied Mathematics 25, Springer New
  York, 2nd ed. 2011, eISBN 978-1-4419-7254-5, xx+437pp). 9 chapters:
  Physical and Geometrical Motivation; Topological Spaces; Homotopy Groups;
  Homology Groups; Principal Bundles; Differentiable Manifolds and Matrix
  Lie Groups; Gauge Fields and Instantons; SU(2) and SO(3); Donaldson's
  Theorem.
- **Vol. 2, *Interactions*** (Applied Mathematical Sciences 141, Springer
  New York, 2nd ed. 2011, eISBN 978-1-4419-7895-0, xii+420pp). 6 main
  chapters: Geometrical Background; Physical Motivation; Frame Bundles and
  Spacetimes; Differential Forms and Integration; de Rham Cohomology;
  Characteristic Classes — plus a substantial closing survey (noted by the
  publisher) of Donaldson-Witten theory and the Witten conjecture, not
  separately itemized in the listing consulted.

## Advanced: Yang-Mills theory, geometric analysis, and four-manifold topology

### The Geometry of Four-Manifolds

S.K. Donaldson, P.B. Kronheimer. Oxford Mathematical Monographs, Oxford
University Press, 1990. ISBN 978-0-19-850269-2. DOI
10.1093/oso/9780198535539.001.0001.

10 chapters: 1. Four-Manifolds; 2. Connections; 3. The Fourier Transform and
ADHM Construction; 4. Yang-Mills Moduli Spaces; 5. Topology and Connections;
6. Stable Holomorphic Bundles over Kähler Surfaces; 7. Excision and Gluing;
8. Non-Existence Results; 9. Invariants of Smooth Four-Manifolds;
10. The Differential Topology of Algebraic Surfaces. Plus an appendix.

### Instantons and Four-Manifolds

Daniel S. Freed, Karen K. Uhlenbeck (with the Mathematical Sciences Research
Institute). MSRI Publications vol. 1, Springer New York, 2nd ed., 1991.
eISBN 978-1-4613-9703-8. xxi+194pp.

12 chapters: Introduction to the First Edition; Glossary; Fake ℝ⁴; The
Yang-Mills Equations; Manifolds of Connections; Cones on ℂP²; Orientability;
Introduction to Taubes' Theorem; Taubes' Theorem; Compactness; The Collar
Theorem; The Technique of Fintushel and Stern.

### The Wild World of 4-Manifolds

Alexandru Scorpan. American Mathematical Society, 2005. ISBN
978-0-8218-3749-8 (also 978-1-4704-6861-3). 609pp.

Organized into parts (confirmed in part; a full chapter-by-chapter listing
is pending closer access to the AMS table of contents, which this session
could not retrieve past AMS's bot protection): the book surveys the
topology of simply-connected, closed, oriented smooth 4-manifolds, from
low-dimensional and piecewise-linear topology through handle-body
constructions to a closing **Part IV, Gauge Theory on 4-Manifolds**,
beginning with **Chapter 9, "Prelude, and the Donaldson Invariants."**
