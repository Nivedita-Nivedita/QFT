## A legitimate stand-in: the original course

The two volumes were compiled and edited from the Institute for Advanced
Study's 1996-97 special year program in Quantum Field Theory, which IAS
hosts freely and directly:

- [Fall term](https://www.math.ias.edu/QFT/fall/index.html)
- [Spring term](https://www.math.ias.edu/QFT/spring/index.html)

Notes are posted per lecture in TeX source, DVI, and PostScript (no PDF;
converting a lecture will be a preliminary step whenever one is chosen).

### Fall term lecturers

- David Kazhdan — Quantum Field Theory (5 lectures)
- Joseph Bernstein — Lectures on Supersymmetry (2 lectures + 4 appendices,
  the latter by Deligne)
- Pierre Deligne — Remedial Lecture on Spinors, and Quantization
- Krzysztof Gawedzki — Lectures on Conformal Field Theory (4 lectures)
- Ludwig Faddeev — Quantum Field Theory (5 lectures)
- Edward Witten — Perturbative Renormalization; Scattering Theory; The Dirac
  Index on Manifolds and Loop Spaces (6 lectures total)
- David Gross — Renormalization Groups (5 lectures)
- Pavel Etingof — Notes on Dimensional Regularization

### Spring term lecturers

- Eric D'Hoker — String Theory (15 lectures)
- Edward Witten — Field Theory (19 lectures)
- Pierre Deligne — Assorted Notes (spinor tables; real vs. complex)
- David Kazhdan — Quantization of Gauge Theories
- Rob Kusner — Mapping Class Group Action on Spin Structures
- Dan Freed — Actions and Reality
- Cumrun Vafa — N=2 Theories in Two Dimensions; Introduction to D-branes
- Andrew Strominger — Kaluza-Klein Compactifications, Supersymmetry, and
  Calabi-Yau Spaces (2 lectures)
- Nathan Seiberg — The Dynamics of N=1 SUSY Theories (with problem sets and
  solutions)
