# Dimension-independent estimates and removal of cutoffs

Source: Glimm-Jaffe, Chapters 10-11, pp. 229-253.

Estimates on the finite-volume `P(φ)₂` measure that do not degrade as the
volume or ultraviolet cutoff is removed, culminating in the existence of the
infinite-volume, cutoff-free field as a monotone/weak limit.
