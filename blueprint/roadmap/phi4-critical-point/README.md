# The φ⁴ critical point

Source: Glimm-Jaffe, Chapter 17, pp. 339-355. This is the project's target
chapter; see [sources/glimm-jaffe.md](../../sources/glimm-jaffe.md) for the
full section list.

Studies the interaction `V(φ) = λφ⁴ + σφ² - μφ` (`λ > 0`) and defines the
critical value `σ_c`: the infimum, at `μ = 0`, of values of `σ` for which the
theory has a unique phase and exponential decay of correlations (equivalently
a spectral gap `m > 0`). Proves existence of `σ_c` and derives bounds on the
associated critical exponents.

Depends on:

- [Correlation inequalities](../correlation-inequalities/README.md) (Lee-Yang
  theorem, p. 339: rules out a transition for `μ ≠ 0`).
- [Phase transitions](../phase-transitions/README.md) (existence of a
  transition for `σ` very negative).
- [The cluster expansion](../cluster-expansion/README.md) (no transition for
  `σ` large).
- [Regularity and axioms](../regularity-and-axioms/README.md) (existence of
  the underlying `P(φ)₂` field these statements are about).

Not yet decomposed into formalizable statements.
