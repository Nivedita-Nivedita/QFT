# Higher-order correlation bounds

Under the hypotheses of the [ξ⁴ inequalities](xi4-inequalities.md), truncated
correlations of even order `≥ 4` are nonpositive (`U(i₁,...,i_v) ≤ 0`, a
special case of `⟨ξ^{A}⟩ ≤ Σ ⟨ξ^{A₁}ξ^{B₁}⟩⟨ξ^{A₂}ξ^{B₂}⟩` summed over
pairings), and for `h_i ≡ 0` an `n`-point function bound holds:

`⟨ξ_{i₁} ⋯ ξ_{i_n}⟩ ≤ (n-1)! Σ_j Σ ⟨ξ_{i₁}ξ_{i₂}⟩ ⋯ ⟨ξ_{i_{2j-1}}ξ_{i_{2j}}⟩⟨ξ_{i_{2j+1}}⟩ ⋯ ⟨ξ_{i_n}⟩`

summed over ways of selecting `j` pairs from `n` objects, proved by induction
on `n` from the twofold Lebowitz-type inequality. These bounds are used later
for the critical exponent estimates of Chapter 17.
