# Moments and singular integrals

Source: Chatterjee-Witten survey [5], Sections 2.7 and 3.4-3.7, pp. 23-24, 29-39.

For bounded `f`, Section 3.4 shows the moments `E[M_{f,ε}(A)^p]` diverge as
`ε → 0` when `p > 1/b²` but converge to finite positive values for `1 ≤ p <
1/b²`. Section 3.5 extends this to `f` with a power-law singularity
`|x-x₀|^{-2αb}`, determining the range of `p` for which the moments converge
(the survey notes this treatment of singular integrals may be new). Section
3.6 shows `M_f(A) = ∞` almost surely once the singularity exponent `α`
reaches the Seiberg bound `α = Q`, which is the probabilistic explanation
(Section 2.7) for why the DOZZ formula vanishes there. Finally, Section 3.7
upgrades the martingale convergence from moments alone to genuine `L¹` (and
then `Lᵖ`) convergence of `M_{f,ε}(A)` for all `b < 1`, completing the
rigorous construction of the Liouville measure.
