# Liouville correlators on the sphere

Source: David, Kupiainen, Rhodes, Vargas, "Liouville Quantum Gravity on the
Riemann Sphere" [1]. See
[sources/liouville/README.md](../../../sources/liouville/README.md).

Uses the Gaussian multiplicative chaos measure from the preceding two
chapters to rigorously define the Liouville correlation functions
`⟨∏ᵢ e^{2αᵢφ(xᵢ)}⟩` on the Riemann sphere, building on Polyakov's original
path-integral proposal. Establishes conformal covariance of the correlators
under the `PSL(2,C)` action, the Seiberg bounds on the `αᵢ`, KPZ scaling, the
KPZ relation, and the Weyl anomaly formula.

Not yet read in the same page-by-page detail as the survey; citations only.
