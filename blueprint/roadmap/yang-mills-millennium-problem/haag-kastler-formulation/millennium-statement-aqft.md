# The Millennium Problem, algebraically

`yang_mills_existence_and_mass_gap_AQFT`: for gauge group data `G`, there
exist a poset of regions, an ambient C*-algebra, a Haag-Kastler net on
them, a time-translation subgroup, and a mass gap `Δ`, such that the net
has that mass gap. The algebraic-picture counterpart of
[the Millennium Problem statement](../millennium-statement/the-conjecture.md).

This is the open Millennium Problem restated, not a proof attempt, and is
`sorry`-terminated for that reason alone — the same way its
Wightman-picture counterpart is.
