# Covariant representations and mass gap

`HaagKastlerNet.CovariantRep`: a `*`-homomorphism `π` of the net's ambient
algebra into bounded operators on a Hilbert space `𝓗`, a vacuum vector, and
unitaries `unitaries : G → (𝓗 →L[ℂ] 𝓗)` implementing `sigma` on the
represented algebra, together with a Hamiltonian `Ham` such that
`unitaries` restricted to a chosen time-translation subgroup `timeMap` is
generated by `Ham` via the ordinary bounded-operator exponential series
(`NormedSpace.exp`).

Unlike [`clustering_from_mass_gap`](../clustering-theorem/clustering.md),
this needs no Stone/SNAG correspondence: `Ham` is given data whose
relationship to the unitaries is asserted directly, not derived from
continuity of an abstract one-parameter group of a possibly-unbounded
generator.

`HaagKastlerNet.HasMassGap net timeMap Δ`: the net admits a covariant
representation whose Hamiltonian has mass gap `Δ`, in the sense already
defined for the [Wightman picture](../axiomatic-qft/mass-gap-definitions.md).
